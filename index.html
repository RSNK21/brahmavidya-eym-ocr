Help & about

Using EyM
  1. Upload a page photo, scan or PDF. MIDF reads it in the EyM cloud (the first read of the day can take about a minute while the cloud wakes up).
  2. Check: point at a word on the page or in the text to see both highlighted. Coloured words are less certain; a dotted underline means the word is not a Monier-Williams headword (inflected forms are often flagged, that's fine).
  3. Fix: click any word to correct it: the whole word, single letters, or its box on the page. EyM learns your fixes.
  4. Refine with AI (optional): add your own ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini key. The AI compares the text with the image and suggests corrections you can review.
  5. Download as text, Word or PDF.
Reading models
cloudMIDF (default): Kraken PP-OCRv6 trained for Sanskrit manuscripts. Fast. Use “MIDF · printed books” for printed pages. EasyOCR reads whole pages. Sansar OCR 2B is a large vision model (slow). TrOCR reads word by word.
computerIn-browser: Shreeshrii Sanskrit Tesseract models and Tesseract.js run on your device with no cloud.
